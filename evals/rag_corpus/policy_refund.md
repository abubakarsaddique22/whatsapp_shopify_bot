# Refund policy

## Return window
You can return a watch within 14 days of delivery.
The watch must be unused, in its original box, with all tags and papers.

## Items we cannot take back
Watches with scratches, a changed strap length, or a missing box cannot be returned.
Engraved or custom-ordered watches are final sale.

## How to start a return
Message us on WhatsApp with your order number and clear photos of the watch.
We reply with return instructions within 1 business day.

## When you get your money
Approved refunds are paid to the original payment method within 7 business days after we receive the watch.
Cash on delivery orders are refunded by bank transfer.

## Return shipping cost
We pay return shipping if the watch arrived damaged or the wrong item was sent.
In every other case the customer pays return shipping.

## Exchanges
You can exchange a watch for another colour or size within 14 days if it is in stock.
