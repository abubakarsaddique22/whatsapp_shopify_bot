# Privacy policy

## What we collect
We collect your name, phone number, delivery address and order details.
We also keep the messages you send us on WhatsApp so we can help you.

## Why we collect it
We use your data only to deliver orders, answer your questions and prevent fraud.

## Sharing
We never sell your personal data.
We share your name, phone number and address only with the courier that delivers your order.

## Payments
We do not store card numbers. Payments are handled by the payment provider.

## How long we keep data
We keep order records for 5 years for tax and warranty reasons.
WhatsApp chat messages are deleted after 12 months.

## Your rights
You can ask us to show, correct or delete your personal data.
Write to us on WhatsApp and we reply within 7 days.

## Cookies
Our website uses cookies to remember your cart and to count visits.
