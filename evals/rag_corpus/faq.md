# Frequently asked questions

Q: Are your watches original and genuine?
A: Every watch we sell is 100% original and comes with the brand box and papers.

Q: Do the watches come with a warranty?
A: Yes. Every watch has a 1 year warranty against manufacturing defects.

Q: How do I claim the warranty?
A: Message us on WhatsApp with your order number and a short video of the problem. We reply within 2 business days.

Q: Which payment methods do you accept?
A: We accept cash on delivery, bank transfer, Easypaisa, JazzCash and card payment at checkout.

Q: Can I change my delivery address after I place an order?
A: You can change the delivery address before the order is dispatched. Message us on WhatsApp with your order number and the new address.

Q: Can I cancel my order?
A: You can cancel an order for free before it is dispatched. After dispatch, please use the return process.

Q: What are your support hours?
A: Our WhatsApp support is open from Monday to Saturday, 10 am to 8 pm. We are closed on Sundays.

Q: How do I find my wrist size?
A: Wrap a paper strip around your wrist, mark where it overlaps and measure it in centimetres. Most of our watches fit wrists from 15 cm to 20 cm.

Q: Do you provide a gift box or a gift card?
A: Yes. Every watch comes in the brand box, and we add a gift bag and a handwritten card for free on request.

Q: Do you give a discount on bulk orders?
A: Yes. For 5 or more watches in one order we give a bulk discount. Message us on WhatsApp for a quote.

Q: Can I swim with my watch?
A: Only watches marked 100 m or 200 m water resistance are safe for swimming. Do not press the buttons under water.

Q: How long does the battery last?
A: A quartz watch battery lasts about 2 years. We replace the battery free of charge during the warranty year.
