# Shipping policy

## Delivery time
Orders are delivered in 3 to 5 working days in major cities.
Other areas take 5 to 7 working days.

## Shipping charges
Shipping is free on orders above Rs 5,000.
Orders below Rs 5,000 pay a flat Rs 250 shipping fee.

## Order processing
We pack and dispatch orders within 24 hours on working days.
Orders placed on Sunday are dispatched on Monday.

## Tracking
You receive a tracking number on WhatsApp once the order is dispatched.

## Cash on delivery
Cash on delivery is available across Pakistan for orders up to Rs 50,000.

## Delivery attempts
The courier tries to deliver twice. After two failed attempts the order is returned to us.

## International shipping
We do not ship outside Pakistan at the moment.
